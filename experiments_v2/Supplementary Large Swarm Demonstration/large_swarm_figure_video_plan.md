executed.

## Figure sequence

Use a three-panel sequence for each authorized showcase: initial parking state,
intermediate transition, and final formation. Prefer one main D1 sequence and a
supplementary D3 sequence. Display N, task family, and simulation provenance.
Do not add fitted scaling plots.

## Supplementary video

- D1: wide camera showing the complete unified Circle transition.
- D2: show the exact natural-language command and an overlay mapping
  qualitative `normal` plus `auto T` to the observed `r_exec` and `T_exec`.

Permitted visualization-only changes are camera angle, model color, background,
labels, and trajectory overlays. They must be identified as visualization-only
and excluded from scientific method identity. UAV dynamics, physics, control,
safety, timing, and task definitions must not change for screenshots or video.

Because the current sweep selected no eligible primary N, this plan remains
contingent on separate human review and a scientifically governed resolution of
the infrastructure boundary. No capture run is currently authorized.
